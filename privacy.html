Privacy Policy for ZenStar Territory

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ZenStar Territory ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

1. Information We Collect

A. Personal Data Provided by You

Account Information: If you choose to sign in via Google Sign-In, we collect basic account information such as your name, email address, and profile picture identifier to manage cloud sync, authentication, and restore your game progress across devices.

B. Automatically Collected Information & Identifiers

  • Device Identifiers & Telemetry: We and our third-party service providers (such as Google Mobile Ads / AdMob and Firebase) may collect unique device identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID), IP addresses, operating system version, device model, and performance metrics to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and maintain app stability.
  • In-App Purchase & Transaction Data: When you make in-app purchases (e.g., hint packs or ad-free upgrades), transactions are processed securely through Google Play Billing. We do not store or receive your credit card details.

C. Game Progress & Local Data

Saved Game Data: Your puzzle progress, completed levels, and settings are stored locally on your device and, if signed in, synchronized with Firebase Cloud Firestore.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Game Functionality: To provide, maintain, and save your game state and puzzle progress.
  • In-App Purchases: To fulfill purchases (e.g., removing ads, delivering purchased hints) through Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising & Ads Personalization: To serve advertisements (including banner and rewarded video ads) via Google AdMob and manage privacy consent via Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Security & Fraud Prevention: To protect against unauthorized access, cheating, or abusive traffic.

3. Third-Party Services & SDKs

Our App integrates services provided by third parties that may collect information sent by your device:

  1. Google Play Services / Google Play Billing: Used for core Android capabilities and processing in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy
  2. Google AdMob & User Messaging Platform (UMP): Used to serve advertisements and manage user consent settings. Google Advertising Policy
  3. Firebase (Authentication & Firestore): Used for cloud save synchronization and security verification. Firebase Privacy Policy

4. Children's Privacy

ZenStar Territory is designed for general audiences and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information without parental consent, please contact us so we can promptly delete such information.

5. Your Data Rights & Choices

  • Ad Choices & Consent: You can adjust your consent choices at any time through in-game settings or your device's privacy settings.
  • Account & Data Deletion: You can request account deletion or data deletion at any time by contacting us at the email provided below.
